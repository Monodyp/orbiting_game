# Ice Ice Water! Product Requirements

## Current direction — 2026-09-28
Ice Ice Water remains a browser freeze-tag game. The conflicting FPS direction in `implementation_plan.md` is obsolete and must not be implemented. This PRD and the current freeze/rescue implementation define gameplay. Product name, guest sessions, private invite rooms, authoritative server, desktop/mobile support, and existing stack remain.

## Playable release
- First-person keyboard/mouse and mobile touch play exclusively on Frostline, an original 120 × 120 metre frozen research arena. Frost Island and Original World implementations and assets remain in the repository but are unavailable to players; rooms default to Frostline and server-side configuration rejects every other map ID.
- Desktop controls: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims with pointer lock, Space jumps, Shift sprints, C slides, RMB lunges, and Control crouches/dives. Left click performs the server-authoritative tag/rescue interaction. Mobile and tablet layouts provide a movement stick, drag-to-look region, dedicated tag/rescue, jump, slide, sprint, and lunge controls, plus crouch and scoreboard controls. Touch surfaces suppress browser gestures while the game is active. Frostline channels are shallow wading water.
- Movement, interaction range, frozen/rescued status, cooldowns, pre-game roles, teams, and match deadlines are server-authoritative. Players have no health, death, elimination, or respawn mechanic. Frozen Water remains on the Water team and can be rescued; frozen is not spectator.
- There are no guns, weapon loadouts, ammunition, hitscan, or damage combat. Ice and Water players can lunge for 0.6 seconds at roughly twice running speed, with a 3-second cooldown and no momentum carryover. The server captures the current validated WASD/touch movement direction when the lunge starts, normalizes diagonal input, and keeps that direction for the lunge; with no movement input it uses the current facing direction. Airborne players can wall-run automatically beside a wall while holding movement and jump away from it to kick off. Ice players freeze a nearby alive Water player by pressing left click within 2 metres, launching them roughly 2 metres backward and 2 metres upward. A Water player unfreezes a nearby frozen Water teammate with the same interaction. Frozen players cannot move; rescued players receive brief protection. Ice wins immediately when every Water player is frozen.
- Ice Ice Water is the only game mode: a five-minute Ice-versus-Water survival match with friendly fire disabled. At the deadline, Water wins only when at least `ceil(starting Water × 0.60)` original Water players are unfrozen; otherwise Ice wins. The original Water-team size is fixed at match start, while the unfrozen count changes immediately after freezes, rescues, and active-match departures.
- The room host selects `Random` or `User picks` team assignment. Before countdown, each player chooses exactly one role: `ICE`, `WATER`, or `SPECTATOR`. Random mode balances only connected ICE/WATER participants and preserves Spectator as explicit opt-in. User Picks honors ICE/WATER choices within balanced quotas; Spectators are never assigned to either team. Invalid values and post-lobby role changes are rejected. The roster shows every connected member's role and marks the local player by authenticated player ID.
- At least two connected ICE/WATER participants are required to start; selected Spectators do not count toward that minimum, Ice quota, Water quota, or win conditions. Private rooms retain a configurable cap of 150 total members.
- Match start places only Ice and Water players at server-selected spawns. A player who selected Spectator remains on `team: none`, cannot send gameplay actions, and watches connected Ice/Water participants through the spectator camera with previous/next target controls. Frozen participants remain eligible viewing targets and remain part of the match. Disconnecting does not convert any role or freeze state.
- Lifecycle: lobby → countdown → playing → finished → intermission → closed. Results remain visible for eight seconds total, then players can create another room. There is no public matchmaking service; Play creates a private room in the selected mode.
- HUD: role, timer, authoritative unfrozen-Water counter, freeze/rescue feedback, hold-Tab/touch scoreboard, ephemeral room chat, and spectator target controls. Desktop hints show `WASD — Move`, `SHIFT — Sprint`, `C — Slide`, `RMB — Lunge`, `Left Click — Tag / interact`, and `Space — Jump`. Enter focuses and sends chat; Escape or clicking elsewhere releases focus, and gameplay input is suppressed while typing. Chat is limited to 160 characters and is not persisted. Ice and Water share the match environment and snowstorm warnings. Ice player models and lobby preview use `#FFFFFF`; Water remains `#43C6D6`, and frozen/protected state colors remain distinct. Settings: sensitivity, FOV, audio, crosshair color, reduced effects, and automatic/touch/mouse control choice.
- The repository-authored music loops continuously across the lobby, matches, results, and subsequent matches. Original synthesized combat and movement cues include positional remote shot/footstep presentation. Audio starts only after a user gesture.
- Match summaries including the Ice Ice Water mode/map and player team/final status are stored transactionally in PostgreSQL. No per-tick state or tokens are persisted.

## Access and reliability
Names are sanitized, temporary sessions signed, room entry invite-only, and all trust boundaries validated and rate-limited. Host departure transfers control. Countdown cancels when no eligible players remain. Fresh joins close at countdown; reconnection is reserved for 20–30 seconds (25 default). HTTPS/WSS is required outside local development.

Support modern WebGL 2 desktop and touch browsers. Target 60 FPS desktop and 30 FPS mobile; publish measured device/capacity limits before release. Input resets on blur, pointer-lock loss, disconnect, and hidden tabs. Prediction/interpolation must never award damage or results. Mobile portrait/landscape layouts must be usable without horizontal overflow.

## Asset policy
Broad arena qualities may inform layout, but gameplay remains freeze-tag. The user authorized reuse of the supplied Island and wooden-house GLBs. Their embedded source/author/license metadata and derivative history are retained in `assets/asset-provenance.md`; the wooden-house source is no longer used by Frostline, while underlying permission documentation remains a release gate for the active Island derivative because its metadata identifies a reupload. Frost Island ships the optimized Fort sector as its central ruin plus original procedural archipelago geometry. The 136.6 MB source named in the build report is not present in the current working tree, so rebuilding or extracting more source sectors is blocked until it is restored. Existing project character assets serve the lobby, while the existing music asset serves the full game experience. No Veck.io site assets were downloaded.

## Scope and release gates
No FPS health, weapons, ammunition, duel mode, solo-start mode, player elimination, respawn, ranked play, store, permanent accounts, public matchmaking, or extra infrastructure. Room chat remains ephemeral. Values are centrally configurable. Public release requires rule/boundary tests, real WebSocket security/reconnection coverage, desktop/mobile browser flows, database checks, staged 20/50/100/150 load measurements, and device performance/accessibility playtests. Unverified gates remain open in `TASKS.md`.
