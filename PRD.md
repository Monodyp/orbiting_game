# Ice Ice Water! Product Requirements

## Approved direction — 2026-09-14
The request to execute `implementation_plan.md` approves replacing freeze-tag with a browser arena FPS. Mobile support was explicitly retained. Previous requirements are historical in `docs/archive/freeze-tag-prd.md` and no longer govern gameplay. Product name, guest sessions, private invite rooms, authoritative server, and existing stack remain.

## Playable release
- First-person keyboard/mouse and mobile touch play on Frostline, an original 120 × 120 metre frozen research arena, and Frost Island, a 160 × 160 metre combat archipelago built around the preserved supplied Fort derivative, and Original World, the restored first colorful 250 ? 250 metre village/forest/crystal/mountain/coast map.
- WASD/arrows move, mouse aims with pointer lock, Space jumps or swims upward, Shift slides, C crouches or dives in deep water, and Control sprints. Left click performs a server-authoritative close-range interaction. Mobile provides a movement stick, drag-to-look region, tag/rescue, jump/swim, slide, scoreboard, crouch/dive, and sprint buttons. Island sea water applies buoyancy and drag, suspends sprint/slide boosts, and keeps a player partially submerged; Frostline channels and Original World river ribbons remain shallow wading water. Original World ocean buoyancy uses its Y=1.5 sea surface, lets players hold crouch to dive and release to float back up, and switches submerged cameras to blue-green fog with muffled audio.
- Movement, interaction range, frozen/rescued status, cooldowns, teams, score, and match deadlines are server-authoritative. No physical player-to-player collision. Health, damage, death, and respawn systems are not part of Ice Ice Water.
- There are no guns, weapon loadouts, ammunition, hitscan, or damage combat. Ice and Water players can lunge for 0.6 seconds at roughly twice running speed, with a 3-second cooldown and no momentum carryover. The server captures the current validated WASD/touch movement direction when the lunge starts, normalizes diagonal input, and keeps that direction for the lunge; with no movement input it uses the current facing direction. Airborne players can wall-run automatically beside a wall while holding movement and jump away from it to kick off. Ice players freeze a nearby alive Water player by pressing left click within 2 metres, launching them roughly 2 metres backward and 2 metres upward. A Water player unfreezes a nearby frozen Water teammate with the same interaction. Frozen players cannot move; rescued players receive brief protection. Ice wins immediately when every Water player is frozen.
- Ice Ice Water is the only game mode: a five-minute Ice-versus-Water survival match with friendly fire disabled. At the deadline, Water wins only when at least `ceil(starting Water × 0.60)` original Water players are unfrozen; otherwise Ice wins. The original Water-team size is fixed at match start, while the unfrozen count changes immediately after freezes, rescues, and active-match departures.
- The private lobby has no team-selection control. At countdown completion the server shuffles all connected humans and development bots, then assigns the nearest whole one-in-five share to Ice (with one Ice player for solo practice); everyone else is Water.
- One connected player may start solo practice (assumption adopted from the plan); two or more are needed for competition. Private rooms retain a configurable cap of 150. The compact arena is not yet validated for 150-player combat.
- Match start places players at server-selected spawns. Brief post-rescue protection lasts 1.5 seconds. Disconnecting does not reset score or deadlines. Reserved disconnected bodies remain frozen or vulnerable to freezing; expired reservations become spectators and cannot rejoin that match.
- Lifecycle: lobby → countdown → playing → finished → intermission → closed. Results remain visible for eight seconds total, then players can create another room. There is no public matchmaking service; Play creates a private room in the selected mode.
- HUD: crosshair, timer, an authoritative `WATER UNFROZEN: current / started` counter visible to both teams, freeze/rescue feedback, and hold-Tab/touch scoreboard. At two minutes remaining, every player sees a brief large warning while Frostline and Frost Island smoothly shift from daylight to a readable cool night over 12 seconds; Original World remains in its authored night state. Snowstorm warnings are shown when applicable. Settings: sensitivity, 90–110 degree FOV, master/music/SFX volume, mute, crosshair color, reduced effects, and automatic/touch/mouse control choice.
- Frostline's presentation-only snowstorm begins at exactly 4:00 remaining without changing the five-minute round deadline. It escalates from a warning and light diagonal snow through short wind gusts, surface accumulation, darkening cloud cover, layered wind audio, and increasingly dense fog into a late playable whiteout. Close players and the center aiming area remain readable; reduced effects and touch use lower particle budgets. The storm never changes movement or collision.
- The repository-authored music loops continuously across the lobby, matches, results, and subsequent matches. Original synthesized combat and movement cues include positional remote shot/footstep presentation. Audio starts only after a user gesture.
- Match summaries including the Ice Ice Water mode/map and player team/final status are stored transactionally in PostgreSQL. No per-tick state or tokens are persisted.

## Access and reliability
Names are sanitized, temporary sessions signed, room entry invite-only, and all trust boundaries validated and rate-limited. Host departure transfers control. Countdown cancels when no eligible players remain. Fresh joins close at countdown; reconnection is reserved for 20–30 seconds (25 default). HTTPS/WSS is required outside local development.

Support modern WebGL 2 desktop and touch browsers. Target 60 FPS desktop and 30 FPS mobile; publish measured device/capacity limits before release. Input resets on blur, pointer-lock loss, disconnect, and hidden tabs. Prediction/interpolation must never award damage or results. Mobile portrait/landscape layouts must be usable without horizontal overflow.

## Asset policy
Veck.io informs broad arena-FPS qualities. The user authorized reuse of the supplied Island and wooden-house GLBs. Their embedded source/author/license metadata and derivative history are retained in `assets/asset-provenance.md`; the wooden-house source is no longer used by Frostline, while underlying permission documentation remains a release gate for the active Island derivative because its metadata identifies a reupload. Frost Island ships the optimized Fort sector as its central ruin plus original procedural archipelago geometry. The 136.6 MB source named in the build report is not present in the current working tree, so rebuilding or extracting more source sectors is blocked until it is restored. Existing project character assets serve the lobby, while the existing music asset serves the full game experience. No Veck.io site assets were downloaded.

## Scope and release gates
No legacy freeze-tag mode, progression, ranked play, store, accounts, chat, physics engine, public matchmaking, or extra infrastructure. Values are centrally configurable. Public release requires rule/boundary tests, real WebSocket security/reconnection coverage, desktop/mobile browser flows, database checks, staged 20/50/100/150 gameplay load measurements, and device performance/accessibility playtests. Unverified gates remain open in `TASKS.md`.
